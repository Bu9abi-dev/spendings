Where the council agrees
• Home has too many competing "left" numbers: 22,489 total, 14,520 free in ADIB, 1,850 left to spend, 86% left. They don't visibly add up (the flow chart says "Kept in ADIB 290"; ADCB shows 3,896 against 1,850 allowance left). Trust in the numbers is the top problem.
• Confirmed bug, found independently by two advisors: the "Left this cycle" card turns from 86% into "AED 88" after any change.
• The floating tab bar and + button cover content ("It landed", last rows), worst on small phones.
• The pace number ("ahead / behind") is the most useful daily figure and is buried in Insights. "Ahead" is shown in red, which reads as bad.

Where it clashes
• Headline number: "Free in ADIB" (First Principles) vs one "Safe to spend" figure that everything else adds up to (Outsider). The reviewers back the Outsider: one number, defined in plain words, with sub-figures that reconcile to it.
• Forecasting and Emirati features (Expansionist) vs fixing what's there first. All five reviewers say fix first; the 30-day ADIB forecast is a strong next step once Home is clear.

Blind spots caught in review
• No one checked the maths itself (pace, rounding, undo for every kind of change), only how it reads.
• Cycle rollover: does unspent allowance carry over, and does a mid-cycle top-up double-count?
• Is the allowance one pot across ADCB and BOTIM?
• A plain "okay / not okay" signal for the 8am glance.
• No test on a real iPhone (installed app, keyboard, safe areas).

Recommendation
Rebuild the top of Home around one number, "Safe to spend", with a one-line definition and pace ("on track", "AED 50 under pace"). Show total money, ADIB emergency and allowance left as labelled parts of it. Take the % card and the flow chart off the first screen. Define the allowance once (from the payday plan). Fix the cheap defects in the same pass:
• the % bug
• the tab-bar overlap
• a tappable "Nafis due" line
• red "ahead"
• the "AD/AD" badges
• giant-number overflow
• stale plan rules when an account is removed
• transfers defaulting the wrong way
• repeat merchants prefilling their last category

First thing to do
Fix the % → "AED 88" bug and the tab-bar overlap. Those are the two defects every user hits.
